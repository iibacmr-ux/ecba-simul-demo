# V2.2 findings and source rules

- The Business Analysis Standard v2.0 states that the six Knowledge Areas and 30 business-analysis task descriptions remain; detailed certification study should use BABOK and the appropriate certification handbooks.
- BABOK v3 explicitly says tasks are not a prescribed process/order. They may be sequential, iterative or simultaneous as long as needed inputs are present.
- Standard v2 task cards use updated labels such as “Trace Requirements and Designs”; these are aliases of BABOK v3 task 5.1 “Trace Requirements”.
- The official French glossary can use “analyse d'affaires”, while the French BABOK translation and BA Quest content commonly use “analyse métier”. Rosine recognizes both.
- Page references now carry both printed-page and physical-PDF-page numbers.
- The four supplied PDFs are source authorities for V2.2 but are not redistributed inside the delta.
